🚀 LUNAR CADETS — J&R ASTROLABS

👨‍🚀 Junior Astronaut Mission Trainer

🌕 Lunar Cadets is an interactive Junior Astronaut Mission Trainer where students experience the journey of becoming an astronaut and completing a lunar mission.

🎮 ABOUT THE GAME

Players progress through astronaut selection, training, mission preparation, launch, landing, lunar outpost operations, rover exploration, and return to Earth.

🧠 The game allows players to make decisions that can affect their mission and learn about space science through gameplay.

🛰️ GAME JOURNEY

1️⃣ ASTRONAUT APPLICATION

📝 Create your astronaut profile with your name, age, country, interests, skills, call sign, specialty, reason for exploring space, avatar, and mission patch.

2️⃣ ASTRONAUT SELECTION

🤖 Complete ASTRA AI aptitude tests including reaction, memory, spatial, observation, and interview challenges.

3️⃣ ASTRONAUT TRAINING

🏋️ Complete five training challenges:

🛰️ Microgravity Retrieval
🔧 EVA Repair
🌕 Lunar Gravity Mobility
🔬 Geology Triage
🚨 Emergency Drill

🏆 Complete all challenges to become Mission Qualified.

4️⃣ MISSION PLANNING

📦 Prepare cargo for a 30-day lunar mission.

⚖️ Choose between food, science equipment, batteries, and spare parts while managing limited mass, power, and volume.

5️⃣ LAUNCH

🚀 Complete the launch checklist and countdown while responding to important events during ascent.

6️⃣ LUNAR LANDING

🌕 Control the lunar lander and attempt a safe landing on the prepared landing pad.

7️⃣ LUNAR OUTPOST

🏠 Survive 30 mission days while managing:

🫁 Oxygen
💧 Water
🍎 Food
🔋 Battery Power
☀️ Solar Panels
🌫️ CO₂ Scrubbers
❤️ Crew Health

⚠️ Handle emergencies such as solar storms and system failures.

8️⃣ ROVER EXPEDITIONS

🤖 Explore the Moon using a lunar rover.

🔬 Collect samples
⛏️ Drill research sites
🧊 Search for subsurface water ice
🔋 Manage rover battery

9️⃣ RETURN & MISSION REPORT

🌎 Return from the Moon, re-enter Earth's atmosphere, and splash down safely.

📊 Receive a personalized mission report covering crew safety, science, resources, engineering, exploration, and mission objectives.
